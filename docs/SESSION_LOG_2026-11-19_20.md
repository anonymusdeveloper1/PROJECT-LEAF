## 2. Your rules and decisions from this session

**Rules, now in `CLAUDE.md`** (the project's rule file, created this session) and in Claude's memory:
- **3D models:**
  - You make every model in Meshy. Claude never models, except the **cat's bows and arrows**, which Claude may design in
    Blender, showing you pictures first.
  - Claude uses Blender only to finish your files (clean-up, colours, rig, animation, export).
  - Concept images are made only when you ask.
- **Nothing new without your yes.** No creatures, mechanics or content you didn't ask for. Each fix is described in plain
  words first. Everything in the game stays unless you ask to remove it.
- **Agents by difficulty:** Haiku for simple jobs, Sonnet for easy-to-medium ones, Opus for normal and hard ones. Each
  agent owns its own files; tests run one at a time.
- **Test lightly, no checker tools or simulations,** and get the build on the phone early.
- **Spell out every choice:** no vague labels like "your split".

**Game decisions:**
- **The progression is a chain:** missions → leaves and timber → buildings → stats and bows → the home's goal list →
  the home grows → the next missions open.
- **No replays:** each mission is won once, and its win pays enough to upgrade.
- **The home is not maxed when the missions end.** Levels 6–8 come after, with the Burrows.
- **Roads:** keep the four roads, with most waves from all of them.
- **Waves:** many enemies in a wave but few on the field, few tanks, and structured squads.
- **Mission 2:** easier, but not a lot.
- **The camp-fall leaf loss stays at 30%.**
- **The Market is offline** (no online auction).
- **Thistle is free.** Paid mounts (looks only) come later.
- **The Saddlery:** after mission 8, earned only, never paid. You can combine as many skills and attachments as you
  want.
- **The Wishing Well** works by itself and is never tapped.
- **The name "the Hearthtree"** for the home and campfire. It can be renamed any time with one key in
  `balance.json`.

## 3. What was built, step by step

### Phase F (19 Nov)
- **Home goals:**
  - each home level lists goals (stats, bow, Training Target, Bow Bench, towers, the mission before won);
  - the next act's first mission needs the new level (missions 3, 7, 11 and 14);
  - steps after missions 2, 6, 10 and 13;
  - the Training Target and Bow Bench go to level 5 (stat and bow caps 4/6/8/9/10);
  - stat and bow prices +30%, Range +0.2 m a level.
- **No replays;** win rewards raised (100 → 2,000 leaves).
- **Roads:** surround, pincer and rush patterns; mission 1 teaches them; the next wave's roads show in the top bar.
- **Waves:** bursts; missions 6, 13 and 15 get real last chapters.
- **Mission 2 and boars:** fewer and softer boars; a boar that misses its charge is dazed and takes 1.5x damage.
- **Reasons to move:**
  - Pitchers and Frogs shoot from 8 / 7.5 m;
  - the Golden Grub: a bonus runner with 40 leaves;
  - the Gloom Totem: a crystal that makes enemies near it stronger until broken;
  - quick, intact and early-call pay bonuses.
- **The Gloom over the woods** fades a step per mission won.

### G1 (20 Nov): the soft-lock and fair waves
- **Relighting the ashes** after the camp falls costs 40 leaves and no timber, and is free with fewer leaves.
- **Rebuilding mid-fight** costs 10 timber, or 60 leaves without timber.
- **Timber:** 1 per wave held (x the pay scale), double chest timber, and the Market Stall sells 5 timber for 50 leaves
  (and buys at 30).
- **Waves:**
  - 10→25 / 16→32 / 20→38 enemies per wave;
  - at most 10 / 12 / 14 on the field and 1 / 2 / 3 tanks at once;
  - "no easy waves" swaps enemies instead of adding them;
  - rush waves are faster, not bigger.

### G1b (20 Nov): squads
- **12 squads with roles** (light, fighter, tank, support), each from a set mission: Scouts, Chief's Band, Boar Raid,
  Brute Wall, Stump Siege, Bandits, Moth Cloud, Moth Escort, Spitter Nest, Choir, Shield Wall, Crystal Creep.
- **Bosses** come with escort squads; elites lead their own small squads.
- **A squad comes down one road in formation:** the leader in front, light ones beside, support behind. It breaks off
  near the camp.
- **Mixing rules:**
  - from mission 2, at least 2 squad types per wave;
  - from mission 3, no kind over about 40% where the squads allow it.

### G2 (20 Nov): the camp
- **The Hearthtree name** everywhere.
- **The pet's Mushroom Cottage and the Market** can be built at Hearthtree level 2, with Thistle free with the Market.
- **The Market:** Bramble sells timber, food and, after the missions, Burrow materials, and buys some back. The Stable
  is on its card.
- **Barricades:**
  - built as **rows of 3** at 12 road-spot markers (no longer sold in the shop);
  - pieces crack, then fall flat with rubble, and enemies pass the gap;
  - tap a row to repair it;
  - old saves converted.
- **Tower repair** on the spot.
- **The Wishing Well is automatic:** it mends everything up to 60 / 75 / 90% between waves and a little each second in
  waves. There is no wishing.
- **Shop-style cards:** item tiles in a grid (icon, name, line, bars, price, one button) with section headings. 32 icons
  are drawn in code.
- **Mission 1's first win** gives 60 leaves instead of barricade pieces.
